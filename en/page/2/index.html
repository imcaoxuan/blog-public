The History of China's Straw Burning Ban Policy: From Traditional Farming to Modern Environmental Governance

The history of China’s straw burning ban policy is, in effect, a microcosm of the country’s agricultural modernization, rural energy transition, environmental governance, and the evolution of grassroots administrative practice. The most important historical conclusion is this: large-scale straw burning was not a problem that existed in China throughout history, nor did the prohibition begin only in recent years.
